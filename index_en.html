MY BACKGROUND

A little about me.

I started studying computer science at Tampere University in 2020. During my studies, I became interested in many topics, from programming fundamentals and databases to usability and accessibility.

Eventually, I decided to focus on programming and web development. I still aim to consider usability and accessibility in everything I do.

HOW I WORK

Using AI

I have used AI both in browser-based chat services and in agent-based tools such as Claude Code and GitHub Copilot.

I have aimed to use AI in different ways: to maintain and develop my independent skills as well as my AI-assisted development skills. I have used AI most often to troubleshoot problems, explore alternatives, and create fictional data, and to some extent to generate code.

I created this site with GitHub Copilot. I tested its accessibility with the Axe DevTools extension, and the site conforms to WCAG 2.1 AA.
